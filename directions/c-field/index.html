Fig. 02Survey · Measure · Project

One scene,read three ways.

02.1Plan

Every dataset in one georeferenced scene.

Models, point clouds, orthomosaics and offline maps load into a single coordinate frame. The tank farm in plan is the same tank farm in 3D, at the same coordinates, on the same timeline.

  • 3D model
  • Point cloud
  • Orthomosaic
  • Offline map
02.2Measure

Volumes that hold up at the next survey.

Stockpiles are measured surface to surface and compared across survey dates. Change is reported per pile, in cubic metres and tonnes, with the base method stated next to the number.

  • Surface to surface
  • Per pile
  • m³ and tonnes
02.3Project

Video on the model.

Drone video is synced to its flight log and projected onto the 3D model from the drone's own position. Pause on a frame and you are looking at the asset, in place, not at a clip.

  • Synced to flight log
  • Projected from drone pose
  • Annotate on the frame

Fig. 05Specification QAI-SPEC-001

Specified forrestricted networks.

QAI-SPEC-001 · Data handlingIssue 0.13
1 Operation
1.1

Runs offline.

The software shall import, process, view and report with no network connection. Offline maps are held with the project.

1.2

No telemetry.

The software shall not transmit usage data, crash reports or analytics. There is nothing to opt out of.

1.3

No account.

Installation and operation shall not require sign-in to any online service.

2 Data
2.1

Data stays on your disks.

Project data shall remain on storage the operator controls: workstation, network share or removable media.

2.2

AI is off by default.

The in-app agent shall stay disabled until the operator enables it, and shall use the operator's own provider key.

2.3

Read-only delivery.

Client packages (.aio) shall open read-only in the free Player.
